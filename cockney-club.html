Cockney Club Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 2026

1. What This Is

The Cockney Club is an annual paid membership offered by Cockney Cards ("we", "us", "our") to customers in the United Kingdom. It gives members 30% off the price of personalised cards, applied automatically at checkout while the membership is active, in exchange for the annual fee shown at the time you join or renew (the "Membership Fee"). These terms sit alongside our general store policies (returns, refunds, and delivery) shown in the footer of this site, which continue to apply to any order you place, Club member or not.

2. Joining

To join the Cockney Club you need a registered Cockney Cards account in good standing, and you must be aged 18 or over. You'll need to be logged in to join, and to receive Club pricing at checkout — the discount is checked against your account each time you pay, not stored on your device, so it can never be applied by mistake to someone else's order.

3. Using Your Membership

Membership runs for 12 months from the date you join, and renews automatically unless you cancel (see Section 5). The 30% discount applies to personalised cards only — it doesn't apply to photo prints, postage, or any other product or fee on your order. Membership is for your own personal use. It isn't intended for business, trade, resale, or wholesale ordering, and we reserve the right to restrict or end a membership we reasonably believe is being used that way.

4. Price Changes

We may change the Membership Fee from time to time. If we do, the new price will only apply from your next renewal onwards — it never changes mid-way through a period you've already paid for. We'll email you at least 30 days before any renewal where the price is changing, so you have time to decide whether to continue.

5. Auto-Renewal & Cancelling

Unless you cancel, your membership renews automatically each year using the payment method on file with our payment provider, Stripe. You can switch off auto-renewal at any time from the Cockney Club tab in My Account — doing so stops future renewals but doesn't refund or shorten the period you've already paid for, which continues to give you the 30% discount until it ends.

If you're a new member, UK consumer law gives you the right to cancel within 14 days of joining for a full refund. If you'd like your Club benefits to start straight away rather than waiting out the 14 days, you can ask us for this — but doing so means you're asking us to begin the service early, which affects how much of a refund you'd be entitled to if you cancel partway through those 14 days (broadly, a fair deduction for the discount you've already used). Outside of this cancellation window, membership fees aren't refundable for the remainder of a paid period, in the same way our standard returns policy doesn't cover services already provided.

6. If We Need To End A Membership

We may suspend or end a membership, and decline to offer a refund for the unused portion, if we reasonably believe it's being used for business/resale purposes against Section 3, obtained fraudulently, or used in a way that breaches our general site terms. Outside of those situations, if we ever need to end the Club scheme itself or a specific membership through no fault of yours, we'll refund you for the unused, full months remaining.

7. Changes To These Terms

We may update these terms occasionally — most often to reflect a change in the law, a change to how the Club works, or to make something clearer. If a change would leave you worse off, we'll let you know before it takes effect, and you'll be free to cancel ahead of your next renewal if you'd rather not continue under the new terms.

8. Contact & Governing Law

Questions about your membership can go to contact@cockneycards.com. These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, without affecting any statutory rights you have as a consumer.
